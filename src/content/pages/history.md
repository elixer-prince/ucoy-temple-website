# Our History

The United Congregation of Yisra'Yah was founded in 2015 when a small group
of believers began meeting regularly to study Scripture in its original
Hebrew and Greek context.

## Early days

What began as a home fellowship of a dozen families grew through word of
mouth and a commitment to teaching the whole counsel of God — from Torah
through the Testimony — without setting aside the covenant signs and
appointed times given to Israel.

## Milestones

- **2015** — First Shabbat fellowship in a private home
- **2017** — Moved to a dedicated worship space
- **2019** — Began observing the full calendar of Moedim (appointed times)
- **2021** — Launched the first Hebrew-language learning group
- **2023** — Published the first set of digital service booklets

## Today

We now number over two hundred families across three states. While our
online presence grew during the pandemic, we have always prioritized
in-person fellowship. This website replaces our earlier Google Site,
keeping the same materials available offline on any phone — because the
Word of God should never be out of reach, whether in the sanctuary or on
a journey.
