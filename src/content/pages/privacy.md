# Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising. What follows is a plain account of what touches your device and where your data goes.

## What we collect

**Page views and interactions.** The site uses **PostHog**, an analytics tool, to count which pages are read, which videos are played, which resources are downloaded, and how the search and contact form are used. This helps the temple understand which materials matter most to the congregation.

**No personal data.** PostHog is configured to collect no IP address. No session recording is enabled. No name, email, or message content is sent to PostHog. The contact form sends your message only to Formspree, which emails it to the temple office.

## What PostHog stores

PostHog assigns a random identifier to your browser, stored in your browser's local storage. This identifier lets PostHog distinguish one browser from another so the temple can see how many unique visitors read the site. The identifier is never sent to the temple and cannot be used to identify you.

PostHog retains event data for one year on its free tier. After that, the data is automatically deleted.

## What else touches your device

**Your theme choice.** Light, dark, or your device setting is remembered on your device only, under the name `ucoy-theme`. It never reaches the temple and never leaves your device.

**PostHog local storage.** PostHog stores its random identifier and a small amount of session data in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is only sent to PostHog when you have an internet connection. If you open the site offline, events are queued locally and sent when the connection returns.

**Cloudflare Pages logs.** The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which keeps its own request logs as every web host does. These logs are not under the temple's control.

## How to opt out

You can opt out of PostHog analytics at any time:

1. Visit [PostHog's opt-out page](https://posthog.com/tutorials/opt-out) and follow the instructions.
2. Alternatively, clear your browser's local storage for this site. This removes the PostHog identifier and any queued events.

Opting out does not affect the site's functionality. You can still read all pages, play videos, and download resources.
