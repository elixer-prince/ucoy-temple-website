# About the United Congregation of Yisra'Yah

The United Congregation of Yisra'Yah is a Messianic congregation rooted in
the Hebrew roots of the faith. We gather to worship in both English and
Hebrew, studying the Tanakh and the Testimony through the lens of the
apostolic and prophetic witness.

## Our name

**Yisra'Yah** (ישראה) — "He contends / prevails with God" — is the name
given in scripture to the people of Israel in their covenant relationship
with the Almighty. We bear this name as a reminder that our identity is
found in the Eternal One and His covenant promises.

## Our mission

We exist to:

- Worship the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in spirit and truth
- Study and proclaim the whole counsel of God, from Torah to Testimony
- Keep the appointed times and holy days as given to Israel
- Build up the body of Messiah in love and truth

## What to expect

Our services blend traditional and contemporary elements. We sing in both
English and Hebrew, read from the weekly Torah portion, and study the
Testament in its Jewish context. Visitors are always welcome — you need
no prior knowledge and no invitation. Simply come as you are.

Come explore the ancient faith that shaped the world.
