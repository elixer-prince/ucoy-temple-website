Shalom Alechem, my brothers and sisters.

Welcome to the Hikal (Temple) of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> and the
Celebration of the Feast of Unleavened Bread (Matzah). This is the seventh yom
(day) of the feast, the day on which the week of our freedom is brought to its
close.

Let us put our hands together and give Yahweh thanks for this Celebration, and
for the Ru'ach that is in our midst.

SHEMA YISRA'YAH, YAHWEH SHADDAY, YAHWEH ECHAD BARUCKH SHEM KH'VOD MAL'CHU-TO LO-LAM VA-ED.

**Leader: Debarim \{Deut 6:4-9\}**

> "Hear, O Yisra'Yah: <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> All-Mighty, <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is one! And you shall love <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your All-Mighty with all your heart, and with all your being, and with all your might.

## Opening Berakah and Prayer

**Opening berakah (blessings)**

**Leader:** With grateful hearts and joyous songs, we stand before You, O Yahweh, stirred anew by the revived memories of Your wondrous redemption in the yomim (days) of yore. Marvelous have been Your works at all times to the children of men. You satisfy the longing soul and the hungry, You fill with tov (good). They that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death cry unto You and are delivered from their affliction.

Abba of freedom, Your children still groan under the burden of cruel taskmasters. Slavery debases their bodies and minds and robs them of the enjoyment of Your bounties. The fear of cruelty and the peril of death blight the souls of men. O break the irons that bind them.

Teach men to understand that by forging chains for others, they forge chains for themselves, that as long as some are in fetters, no one is truly free. Help them to see that liberty is the very breath of life and that only in the atmosphere of freedom can truth, prosperity and peace flourish. Imbue us with courage to guard our heritage of freedom above all material goods and to preserve it for others so that all men shall dwell together in safety and none shall make them afraid. HalleluYah.

**Congregation:** Our Abba and Master! As the week of Pesach draws to its close, we come into Your house in the knowledge that where You are, there is unending life and hope, liberty and peace. You revive the spirit of the downcast and lift up from the very gates of death. Bestow Your blessing upon us and grant renewal of courage to hearts that have grown weary in the battle for justice. Strengthen their faith in the ultimate triumph of the right. Let Your light shine into our spirits and liberate us and all the children of men from fear and anguish. May the hope of springtime and the promise of freedom find speedy fulfilment in a splendorous harvest of life enriched and redeemed. HalleluYah.

## Berakah and Teaching of the Torah

**Leader:** BARUKH ATAH YAHWEH ABBA MELEKH HA-O-LAM, ASHER BANU MIKHAL HA-A-MIM V'NATAN LANU ET TORATO. BARUKH ATAH YAHWEH, NO-TEIN HA-TORAH.

**Congregation:** Praised are You, Yahweh our Father, Sovereign of the universe, who chose us from all the peoples by giving us Torah. Praised are You, Yahweh, giver of the Torah.

## Closing Prayer

O Yahweh our Abba, on this closing eve of the feast of our freedom, we gather to thank You for the blessings which it has bestowed and for the message it has brought. Delivered by Your hand from the tyrant's might, Yisra'Yah sang a song of praise unto You by the shore of the Red Sea. Many times since hast You saved us from the fury of men and from the floods of destruction. In peril and in need hast You been our tower of strength.

O continue to redeem us and all Your children from danger and oppression. Wherever men groan under the yoke of servitude, hasten to deliver them. Cleanse the hearts of men and their rulers of the passions of hate and strife, of greed and lust for power, and fill them with tov (good) will and the love of justice. Speed the Pesach of the future, when tyranny and slavery shall cease, when sword and spear shall be broken, and freedom and peace shall reign forever. On that yom (day), all men shall come to know that they are brothers, and with gladness and song proclaim You their Abba and Redeemer. HalleluYah.
