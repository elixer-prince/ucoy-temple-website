We gather on Shabbat morning to pray, to read from the Torah, and to study the
Testimony. The order below is the order the service runs in, so you can follow
along from a phone at home or in the sanctuary.

## Opening prayers

The service opens with the morning blessings and the Psalms of praise. We sing
the opening psalm in Hebrew and in English, and the whole congregation is
welcome to join.

### The Shema and its blessings

Before the silent prayer we recite the Shema with the blessings that surround
it. The Hebrew is read aloud, with the English following.

<div lang="he" dir="rtl">

שמע ישראל יהוה אלהינו יהוה אחד
ואהבת את יהוה אלהיך בכל לבבך ובכל נפשך ובכל מאדך
והיו הדברים האלה אשר אנכי מצוך היום על לבבך

</div>

The English of the same words:

> Hear, O Israel: the LORD our God, the LORD is one. Love the LORD your God
> with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might. And these
> words which I am commanding you today shall be upon your heart.

The Hebrew is chanted first, then the whole Congregation answers together in
English, so a visitor who does not read Hebrew can still follow the beat of
the service.

We sing the psalm of the day, <span dir="auto">מזמור ליום השבת</span>, before we go on to the
Torah reading.

## Torah reading

The weekly parashah is read from the scroll, with the blessings before and
after. The reading is chanted in Hebrew and then read again in English.

<Video src="/videos/shabbat-torah.mp4" caption="Torah reading for this week" />

## Teaching and closing

A short teaching from the Testimony follows the reading, then we close with the
Kaddish and a prayer for the peace of Yerushalayim. Kiddush and a shared meal
follow the service, and visitors are always welcome to stay.
