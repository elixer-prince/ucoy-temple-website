## The Shabbat Morning Abodah

It is said in Bereshith: \{Genesis 2:1-3\}

> Thus, the Shamayim (Heavens) and the Erets (Earth) were completed, and all their array.
>
> And on the seventh yom (day) <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> completed His work which He had done, and He rested on the seventh yom from all His work which He had made. And <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> baruk (blessed) the seventh yom (day) and set it apart, because on it, He rested from all His work which <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> in creating had made.

Shemoth: \{Exodus 31:12-15\}

> And <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> spoke to Mosheh (Moses) saying. “And you, speak to the children of Yisra’Yah (Israel), saying, ‘My Sabbaths (Shabbatot) you are to guard, by all means, for it is a sign between Me and you throughout your generations, to know that I, <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, am setting you apart.’
>
> ‘And you shall guard the Shabbat, for it is set-apart to you. Everyone who profanes it shall certainly be put to death, for anyone who does work on it, that being shall be cut off from among his people.’
>
> ‘Six yomim (days) work is done, and on the seventh yom (day) is a Shabbat of rest, set-apart to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>. Everyone doing work on the Shabbat yom (day) shall certainly be put to death.’”

## Opening Prayer

**Leader:** The breath of every living being shall baruk (bless) Your Name, O Yahweh All-Mighty, and the Ruach of all flesh shall continually honour and exult Your memorial, O our King. For You are from everlasting to everlasting. You Are the All-Mighty; and beside You we have neither king, redeemer nor saviour to redeem, deliver, answer and show compassion on us in all times of trouble and distress. We have no king to aid and support us but You.

**Congregation:** ‘Yahweh of the first and of the Last’, ‘Creator of all Creatures’, ‘Father of all Generations’, who is exalted with all manner of praise; who guides His world with tenderness, and His creatures with compassion. And the All-Mighty is ever awake, He slumbers not, and He sleeps not, but it is You who rise us who sleep, revive the dead, heal the sick, open the eyes of the blind, and raise those who are bowed down. You make the dumb to speak, and reveal the hidden things; and therefore, unto You alone do we render adoration, Hallelu’Yah!

### Tehillim 90

1. <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, You have been our refuge in all generations.
2. Before the mountains were born, or You had brought forth the Erets (Earth) and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting You are the All-Mighty.
3. You turn man back to dust, and say, “Return, oh children of men.”
4. For a thousand years in Your eyes are like emesh (yesterday) that has past, or like a watch in the laylah (night).
5. You have swept them away; they are as a sleep, like grass that springs up in the morning.
6. In the morning it flourishes and springs up, at evening it is cut down and withered.
7. For we have been consumed by Your displeasure, and by Your wrath we are alarmed.
8. You have set our crookedness before You, our secret sin in the light of Your face.
9. For all our yomim (days) have passed away in Your wrath, we spend our years like a whisper.
10. The yomim (days) of our lives are seventy years; or if due to strength, eighty years, yet the best of them are but toil and exertion; for it is soon cut off, and we fly away.
11. Who knows the power of Your displeasure? And your wrath, according to the fear of You?
12. Teach us to number our yomim (days) and let us bring the heart to wisdom.
13. Return, O <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>! How long? And be sorry for Your servants.
14. Satisfy us in the morning with Your kindness, and let us sing for simcha (joy) all our yomim (days)!
15. Give us simcha (joy) according to the yomim (days) You have afflicted us, the years we have seen evil.
16. Reveal Your work to Your servants, And Your splendour to their children.
17. And let the pleasantness of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> our Abba (Father) be upon us and confirm the work of our hands for us; O confirm the work of our hands!

### Tehillim 91

1. He who dwells in the secret place of the Most-High, who abides under the shadow of the All-Mighty.
2. He is saying of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, “My refuge and my stronghold, My Abba (Father), in whom I trust!”
3. For He delivers you from the snare of a trapper, from the destructive pestilence.
4. He covers you with His feathers, and under His wings you take refuge; His truth is a shield and armour.
5. You are not afraid of the dread by laylah (night), of the arrow that flies by yom (day),
6. Of the pestilence that walks in darkness, of destruction that ravages at tsohar (midday).
7. A thousand fall at your side, and ten thousand at your right hand; but it does not come near you.
8. Only with your eyes you look on and see the reward of the wrong ones.
9. Because you have made <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> — My refuge, the Most-High — your dwelling place,
10. No evil befalls you, and a plague does not come near your tent.
11. For He commands His malak (messengers) concerning you, to guard you in all your ways.
12. They bear you up in their hands, lest you dash your foot against a stone.
13. You tread upon lion and cobra, young lion and serpent you trample under foot.
14. “Because he cleaves to Me in love, Therefore I deliver him; I set him on high, because he has known My name.”
15. “When he calls on Me, I answer him; I am with him in distress; I deliver him and esteem him.”
16. “With long life I satisfy him and show him My deliverance.”

3. On ten strings, and on the harp, to the sounding chords of the lyre.
4. For You have made me rejoice with Your work, O <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, I shout for simcha (joy) at the works of Your hands.
5. O <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, how great are Your works! Your thoughts are very deep!
6. A senseless man does not know, and a fool does not understand this.
7. When the wrong springs up like grass, and all the workers of wickedness blossom, it is for them to be destroyed forever.
8. But You, <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, are on high forever.
9. For look, Your enemies, O <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, for look, Your enemies do perish; All the workers of wickedness are scattered.
10. But You lift my horn like a wild ox; I have been anointed with fresh oil.
11. And my eye looks upon my enemies; My ears hear the evildoers Who rise up against me.
12. The righteous one flourishes like a palm tree; He grows like a cedar in Lebanon.
13. Those who are planted in the House of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> flourish in the courts of our Abba (Father).
14. They still bear fruit in old age; they are fresh and green,
15. To declare that <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is straight, my Rock, and in Him is no unrighteousness.

## The Ten Words

**The Written Torah (Laws)**

Shemoth: (Exodus 20:1-17) It reads as follows.

1. And Yahweh spoke all these Words, saying,
2. “I am <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Mighty One, who brought you out of the land of Mitsrayim (Egypt), out of the house of slavery.”
3. “You have no other mighty ones against My face.”
4. “You do not make for yourself a carved image, or any likeness of that which is in the Shamayim (the Heavens) above, or which is in the Erets (Earth) beneath, or which is in the waters under the Erets (Earth). You do not bow down to them nor serve them. For I, <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba (Father), am a jealous Abba visiting the crookedness of the fathers on the children to the third and fourth generations of those who hate Me, but showing kindness to thousands, to those who love Me and guard My commands.”
5. “You do not bring the Name of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba (Father) to naught, for <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> does not leave the one unpunished who brings His Name to naught.”
6. “Remember the Shabbat yom (Sabbath day), to set it apart.”
7. “Six yomim (days) you labour, and shall do all your work, but the seventh yom (day) is a Shabbat of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba (Father). You do not do any work — you, nor your son, nor your daughter, nor your male servant, nor your female servant, nor your cattle, nor your stranger who is within your gates.
8. “For in six yomim (days) <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> made the Shamayim (the Heavens) and the Erets (Earth), the sea, and all that is in them, and rested the seventh yom (day). Therefore, <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> baruk (blessed) the Shabbat yom (Sabbath day) and set it apart.”
9. “Respect your father and your mother, so that your yomim (days) are prolonged upon the soil which <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba (Father) is giving you.”
10. “You do not murder.”
11. “You do not commit adultery.”
12. “You do not steal.”
13. “You do not bear false witness against your neighbour.”
14. “You do not covet your neighbour’s house; you do not covet your neighbour’s wife, nor his male servant, nor his female servant, nor his ox, nor his donkey, or whatever belongs to your neighbour.”
