## Berakah before Lighting the Menorah

**Barukh atah Yahweh, Abba, melekh ha-olam, asher kidishanu b'mitz'votav
v'tzivanu l'had'lik neir shel Shabbat.**

Praised are You, Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the universe, who has set us
apart with His commandments and commanded us to light the lights.

## Selection from the Scriptures

**Leader (Ecclesiastes 9:10):** All that your hand finds to do, do it with your
might; for there is no work or planning or knowledge or wisdom in She'ol, the
grave, where you are going.

but the Word of Yahweh stands forever.

## Pesach Prayers

**Leader:** Abba (Father) of all generations, the past is Your handiwork and the
splendours of the future Your promise. On this festival of Pesach, we are
gathered in Your house to recall Your wonders of old. When we were slaves in
Mitsrayim and suffered under the lash of taskmasters, Thou did send Your
servants, Mosheh and Aaron, to stand in the presence of Pharaoh and proclaim in
Your name: Let My people go that they may serve Me.

The cruel heart of the tyrant and the might of his armies did not avail against
Your liberating word, and Yisra'Yah marched forth from Mitsrayim with songs and
rejoicing to find freedom in Your service. This was Your will in ancient yomim
(days), and it is Your law forever.

Wherever the yoke of serfdom is broken, wherever tyrants are overthrown,
wherever Your children live as free men, there is Your word fulfilled and Your
will triumphant. To every soul comes Your command: Be servants unto Me alone,
and not slaves to man.

Teach us, O Yahweh, to accept and cherish Your Torah (law) of liberty. Guide us
with Your wisdom that we may grow in knowledge and in sympathy, and keep our
judgment free from passion or prejudice. May we strive for the redemption of our
souls and thus become messengers of freedom to all Your children.

In Your power, O Abba, is the fate of nations. Yahweh who did break Pharaoh's
yoke and bid the slaves go free, be Thou our help in every time of peril. Let
all men see that the might of armies and the power of tyrants must vanish like
smoke before the force of Your spirit. May the coming year bring a new Pesach of
freedom to all Your children in every land. Hallelu'Yah.

**Congregation:** O Yahweh of the universe, Thou dost manifest Your creative
power in the world about us. We are bidden to observe this season when Thou
didst redeem our fathers from the slavery of Mitsrayim, that they might go
forth to serve Thee in freedom. Help us to cast off the chains that bind our
spirits, and to free ourselves from the selfishness and indifference that keep
us from Thee. May there be release within our souls, an awakening of love for
Thee.

Grant, O Yahweh, that Your people Yisra'Yah may be freed from the tyranny that
sorely besets them, and from the sorrow and despair that burden their hearts.
Redeem us, O Yahweh, from the serfdom of ignorance and irreverence, that we may
continue to fulfil our historic task as Your servants and witnesses. Strengthen
our hearts with unswerving faith and serene trust in Your providence. Thus shall
we become champions of justice upon earth.

Fervently we pray for the universal springtide in the life of mankind, when the
long winter of intolerance and hatred shall have passed, the vision of the
prophets fulfilled and the splendour of Your kingdom acknowledged of all men.

## Kiddush: The Cup of Sanctification

We are now ready to begin the Kiddush, the cup of separation, which means to
set-apart or to make sacred. When Yahweh the Most High said that He would take
Yisra'Yah out of Mitsrayim (Egypt), He separated them.

Taking the goblet in his hand, the leader chants:

**Baruch attah Yahweh Shaddey, Melech ha-olam, boray priy ha-gefen. Baruch
attah Yahweh Shaddey, Melech ha-olam, asher bacher banoo mikol am vero-mama-noo
mikol lashon veki-desha-noo bemitsvotav vati-ten lanoo Yahweh Shaddey be-ahava
mo-adim lesim-cha chagim ooz-manim lesason et yom chag hamat-sot huzeh zeman
cheyrootaynoo be-ahava mikra kodesh zaycher liytsiyat mitsrayim. Kiy vanoo
vacharta ve-otano kidashta mikol ha-amim oomo-oday kodshecha be-ahava
ooveratson besimcha oove-sason hinchaltanoo. Baruch attah Yahweh mekadesh
Yisra'Yah vehaz-mamim. Baruch attah Yahweh Shaddey, Melech ha-olam,
she-he-che-yano vekiy-manoo vehi-gi-yanoo laz-man huzeh.**

Praised are You, Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the universe, who creates the
fruit of the vine. Praised are You, Yahweh Shaddey, Sovereign of the universe,
who has chosen us from all peoples, and hast exalted us above all tongues, and
hast sanctified us by Your commandments.

And Thou hast given us, O Yahweh our Abba, in love, Sabbaths for rest and
anniversaries for rejoicing, festivals and seasons for gladness, this feast of
Pesach, the season of our freedom, in love, a Set-apart convocation, a memorial
of our departure from Mitsrayim. For in us has Thou chosen, and Thou hast
set-apart us above all peoples, and Thou hast given us as heritage Your
Set-apart seasons in love and in favour, in simchah (joy) and in gladness.

Praised are You, O Yahweh, who set-apart Yisra'Yah and the festive seasons.
Praised are You, O Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the universe, who has preserved
us and sustained us and enabled us to reach this season. AlleluYah.

## The Four Cups

Four cups of wine are used in the seder, symbolizing four distinct promises
made by Yahweh, as mentioned in Shemoth (Exodus 6:6-7). These are traditionally
referred to as follows:

- **Cup of separation:** I will bring you out of Mitsrayim (Egypt)
- **Cup of deliverance:** I will deliver you from slavery
- **Cup of redemption:** I will redeem you with My power
- **Cup of restoration:** I will take you as My people

Then drink the wine in a leaning position, after which wash your hands, but do
not say the blessing.

## The Parsley

The Master of the House then takes parsley, or chervil, and dips it into
vinegar, or salt water, and having distributed it, gives some to everyone at the
table. They all say the following grace before they eat it:

**Praised are You, Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the universe, who creates the
fruit of the arets (earth).**

**Leader:** Barukh attah Yahweh Shaddey, melekh ha-olam, nethan lechem erets.

**Congregation:** Praised are You, Yahweh All-Mighty, Sovereign of the
universe, who giveth bread from the earth.

## The Bread of Affliction

The Master of the House then places the matzah in the dish, fills again the
glass with wine, and says:

This is the bread of affliction which our ancestors ate in the land of
Mitsrayim. Let all those who are hungry enter and eat thereof, and all who are
needy, come and celebrate the Pesach (Passover). At this season, next year, may
the whole house of Yisra'Yah be free!

## The Four Questions

The youngest member asks:

**Leader:** Why is this night different from all other nights?

**Congregation:** Why is this night different from all other nights?

**Leader:** On all other nights we eat either leavened or matzah; on this night, only matzah.

**Congregation:** On all other nights we eat either leavened or matzah; on this night, only matzah.

**Leader:** On all other nights we eat herbs of any kind; on this night only bitter herbs.

**Congregation:** On all other nights we eat herbs of any kind; on this night only bitter herbs.

**Leader:** On all other nights we do not dip even once; on this night we dip twice.

**Congregation:** On all other nights we do not dip even once; on this night we dip twice.

**Leader:** On all other nights we eat sitting upright; on this night we all lean.

**Congregation:** On all other nights we eat sitting upright; on this night we all lean.

Had He executed judgment upon their gods without slaying their first born, that alone would have sufficed us. When we were slaves to Pharaoh in Mitsrayim, Yahweh our Abba brought us forth from thence with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm. And if the Set-apart One, praised be He, had not brought forth our ancestors from Mitsrayim, we, and our children, and our children's children, would still have continued in bondage to the Pharaohs in Mitsrayim. Therefore, even if we were all learned, all men of understanding, all of us having knowledge in the Torah, it would nevertheless be incumbent upon us to recount the departure from Mitsrayim, and it is accounted praiseworthy to discourse at length thereon.

## The Ten Plagues

These are the ten plagues which the Set-apart One, praised be He, brought on Mitsrayim (Egypt):

Blood, Frogs, Lice, Flies, Murrain, Boils, Hail, Locusts, Darkness, Slaying of the First Born.

## The Pesach Symbols

- **Pesach:** the Passover
- **Matzah:** the Unleavened
- **Maror:** the Bitter Herbs

## Tehillim 113 and 114

Then all read together the blessing over the wine:

**Baruch attah Yahweh Shaddey, melekh ha-olam, boray priy ha-gefen.**

Praised are You, Yahweh Shaddey, Sovereign of the universe, who creates the fruit of the vine.

Then drink the second glass of wine.

## The Pesach Meal

The Pesach meal will be served.

**Baruch hoo oovarooch shemo. Baruch attah Yahweh Shaddey, melekh ha-olam, hazan et ha-olam coolo betoovo bechen bechesed ooverachamim hoo noten lechem lechol bassar kiy le-olam chasdo, oovetoovo hagadol tamid lo chas-sar lanoo ve-al yechsar lanoo mazon le-olam va-ed ba-avoor shemo hagadol. Kiy hoo zan oomefarness lakol oomavtiv lakol oomaychin mazon lechol beriy-otav asher bara. Baruch attah Yahweh hazan et hakol. Yahweh oz le-amo yiten, Yahweh yevarech et amo vashalom.**

Blessed is He, and blessed be His name. Blessed are You, O Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the universe, who feeds the whole world with His goodness, with grace, with loving-kindness and with mercy. He gives food to all flesh, for His kindness endures forever. And in His great goodness we have never lacked, and may we never lack food for ever and ever, for the sake of His great name. For He feeds and sustains all, and does tov (good) to all, and prepares food for all His creatures which He created. Blessed are You, O Yahweh, who provides food for all.

Yahweh will give strength to His people; Yahweh will bless His people with shalom (peace).

## The Third Cup and the Opening of the Door

The third cup: drink the wine after saying the blessing.

Open the door and say the following:

Of old, our ancestors looked forward to the coming of EliYah to herald the Mashianic Age. Hayom (today) we pledge ourselves to work for justice, love and brotherhood to prevail among mankind, and we open our door that all the world may know it.

## The Fourth Cup

Take the fourth cup.

The Celebrant lifts the fourth cup of wine and says:

**Chasal siddur Pesach kehilchato, kechol mishpato vechukkato.**

The commemoration of the Pesach is now accomplished, according to its order and its prescribed ceremonies. May our prayers be acceptable to the All-Mighty Yahweh. With songs of praise we have lifted up the cup of wine symbolizing His Divine promises. May He who establishes shalom (peace) in His high places bestow through His infinite mercy shalom (peace) upon us and upon all mankind. So be it.

## The Aaronic Berakah

**Leader: Yish-va-re-ch'cha Yahweh va'yish'ma're-cha. Ya-eir Yahweh pa-nav ei-le-cha vi-chu-ne-cha. Yis-sa Yahweh pa-nav ei-le-cha v'ya-seim l'cha shalom.**

Yahweh bless you and keep you; Yahweh make His face shine upon you and be tov (good) to you; Yahweh lift up His countenance upon you and give you shalom (peace).
