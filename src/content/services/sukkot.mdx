Shalom my brothers and sisters!

Welcome to the Hikal (Temple) of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> and the Sukkot Celebration.

In Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 23:

33. And <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> spoke to Mosheh, saying,
34. Speak to the children of Yisra'Yah, saying, 'On the fifteenth yom (day) of this seventh new month is the Festival of Sukkot for seven yomim (days) to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>.
35. 'On the first yom is a set-apart gathering, you do no servile work.
36. 'For seven yomim (days) you bring an offering made by fire to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>. On the eighth yom there shall be a set-apart gathering for you, and you shall bring an offering made by fire to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>. It is a closing festival, you do no servile work.
37. 'These are the appointed times of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> which you proclaim as set-apart gatherings, to bring an offering made by fire to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, an ascending offering and a grain offering, a slaughtering and drink offerings, as commanded for every yom,
38. besides the Shabbaths of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, and besides your gifts, and besides all your vows, and besides all your voluntary offerings which you give to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>.
39. 'On the fifteenth yom of the seventh new month, when you gather in the fruit of the land, celebrate the festival of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> for seven yomim. On the first yom is a rest, and on the eighth yom a rest.
40. 'And you shall take for yourselves on the first yom the fruit of tov trees, branches of palm trees, twigs of leafy trees, and willows of the stream, and shall rejoice before <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba for seven yomim.
41. 'And you shall celebrate it as a festival to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> for seven yomim in the year, a law forever in your generations. Celebrate it in the seventh new month.
42. 'Dwell in booths for seven yomim; all who are native born in Yisra'Yah dwell in booths,
43. so that your generations know that I made the children of Yisra'Yah dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Mitsrayim. I am <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba.' "

44. Thus did Mosheh speak of the appointed times of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> to the children of Yisra'Yah.

Sukkot (tabernacles) is the seven yom (day) festival when Yisra'Yah dwells in a sukkah, an outdoor hut of temporary construction with a roof of branches, most notably eating all of our meals there. This is to commemorate Yahweh's kindness in shielding our ancestors in the Sinai desert. Hayom (today) we are here to celebrate this custom as the Yisra'lites and Yahudim (Jews) did. Let us put our hands together as we give Yahweh Most High thanks for this occasion.

**Congregation:** Praised are You, Yahweh All-mighty, King of the universe, who forms light and creates darkness, who makes peace and creates all things.

## Opening Prayer

**Leader:** All shall thank You and all shall praise You, and all shall declare that there is none set apart like You. All shall extol You forever, O Yahweh who forms all things. O Yahweh All-mighty, who opens every yom (day) the doors of the gates of the East, and cleaves the windows of the firmament, bringing forth the sun from its palace and the moon from the place of its dwelling, and giving light to the whole world and its inhabitants whom You created by Your attribute of pardon.

In pardon You illuminate the earth and those who dwell thereon, and in Your excellence every yom constantly renews the work of creation. "How great are Your works, O Yahweh All-Mighty; in wisdom have You made them all: the earth is full of Your possessions."

O King, who alone was exalted from aforetime; praised and extolled from yomim of old. O everlasting All-Mighty Yahweh, in Your abundant pardon have compassion on us. O Yahweh Most High of our strength, Rock of our fortress, Shield of our salvation, be You a defence for us.

There is none to be compared with You; nor is there anyone beside You; there is none but You; and who is like unto You? There is none to be compared with You, O Yahweh All-Mighty, in this world; nor is there anyone beside You, O our King, in the world to come. There is no other save You, O our Redeemer, in the yomim of the Mashiyach; and who will be like unto You, O our Saviour, at the resurrection of the dead?

**Congregation:** Yahweh is All-Mighty of all works; barak is He and ever to be barak by the mouth of every soul. His greatness and excellence fill the universe. Knowledge and understanding surround Him; He exalts Himself above the set apart ones and is adorned in splendor above the celestial chariot. Purity and rectitude are before His throne, loving-commitment before His splendor.

## Prayer of Thanksgiving

**Leader:** On this yom (day) we call with grateful hearts to You, the Mighty Yahweh who guided our fathers in their wanderings through the barren desert and the trackless wilderness. Under Your protection the weary pilgrims found shelter and security. In famine You sustained them and from the flinty rock You quenched their thirst.

We thank You that the same unfailing compassion has guided and sheltered us, their children, in all the years of our pilgrimage. Our hearts are filled with gratitude at the remembrance of Your excellence. We pray You that the enjoyment of Your berakah (blessing) may awaken within us a spirit of contentment and courage, that we may neither grow proud through success nor become embittered by failure. May we sympathize with those whose hopes have been disappointed and whose labors have been unfruitful. May our hands be outstretched to those who suffer, and our hearts be opened to those who are in need. Praised be You, O Yahweh, giver of all excellence. AlleluYah.

## Shemini Atzeret: The Last Day of Sukkot

**Shemini Atzeret First Prayer: The Last Day of Sukkot**

**Leader:** Almighty Yahweh, who led our fathers through the wilderness and brought them to the Promised Land, we give thanks unto you for your providential care. From old and to this day, you caused our fathers to dwell within the shelter in peace and guided them with a pillar of cloud by day and a pillar of fire by night. You led them from darkness to the light of your worship. As you poured out upon them the gifts of your bounty, You bestowed upon them the blessing of your law that they would seek after truth and strive to do justice, to love mercy and to walk humbly with you.

We beseech you to teach us also to emulate your own grace and mercy. You give us our food in due season, may we learn to share with the needy and to shelter the stranger. May your truth be engraved upon our hearts.

**Congregation:** Send unto us each year a deeper understanding of your word. Cause your truth to fall upon our hearts like seed upon fertile ground, that again and again may the faith of the patriarchs, the vision of the lawgivers and the fervour of the prophets be renewed within us. Grant, O Father, that we may learn to love your law ever more deeply, and to find it in the beauty of set-apartness. Make this a day of sacred joy unto us, so that from our hearts and from the hearts of all men may rise the song: This is the day which Yahweh has made. Let us be glad and rejoice in it. HalleluYah!

**Shemini Atzeret Second Prayer**

**Leader:** Into your gates, O Father and King, we enter with thanksgiving and into your courts with praise. We bless your name, for you are from everlasting to everlasting. Out of chaos, you called the world into being and set upon it the seal of your law. You endowed creation with purpose and beauty. You kindled your light, that we might rejoice in the beauty of your world and the majesty of your ways.

**Congregation:** On this day, O Father, we conclude the set-apart season in reverence and joy. The mighty accents of the shofar, calling us to repentance and to atonement, have stirred our hearts. From the world to you from within, we turned to the world without and lift grateful hearts to you for your unfailing bounty, for the harvest of fruit and grain with which you blessed us. Continue to bestow your benediction upon us and teach us to seek you ever more within the sanctuary of our souls, within the tabernacle of your world, and in the congregation of your worshippers. HalleluYah!
