## Selection from the Scriptures

In the book of Wayyiqra (Leviticus 23:4-8):

> 'These are the appointed times of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, set-apart gatherings which you are to proclaim at their appointed times.
> 'In the first new moon, on the fourteenth day of the new moon, between the evenings, is the Pesach to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>.
> 'And on the fifteenth day of this new moon is the Festival of Matzot to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>: seven days you eat unleavened bread.
> 'On the first day you have a set-apart gathering, you do no servile work.
> 'And you shall bring an offering made by fire to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> for seven days. On the seventh day is a set-apart gathering, you do no servile work.'"

Let us put our hands together and give Yahweh thanks for this celebration, and the Ruach that is in our midst.

The National Anthem: Yah'maica (Jamaica).

The National Anthem: Yisra'Yah (Israel).

## The Shema

SHIMA YISRA'YAH, YAHWEH SHADDAY, YAHWEH ECHAD BARUCKH SHEM KH'VOD MAL'CHU-TO LO-LAM VA-ED.

**Leader: Debarim (Deut 6:4-9)**

> "Hear, O Yisra'Yah: <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> All-Mighty, <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is one!
> "And you shall love <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your All-Mighty with all your heart, and with all your being, and with all your might.
> "And these Words which I am commanding you hayom (today) shall be in your heart,
> And you shall impress them upon your children, and shall speak of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up, and shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.
> "And you shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates.

**Debarim (Deut 11:13-21)**

> 'And it shall be that if you diligently obey My commands which I command you hayom (today), to love <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba and to serve Him with all your heart and with all your being,
> Then I shall give you the rain for your land in its season, the early rain and the latter rain, and you shall gather in your grain, and your new wine, and your oil.
> 'And I shall give grass in your fields for your livestock, and you shall eat and be satisfied.
> 'Guard yourselves, lest your heart be deceived, and you turn aside and serve other mighty ones and bow down to them.
> 'Then the displeasure of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> shall burn against you, and He shall shut up the shamayim (heaven), and there be no rain, and the land not give its increase. And you shall perish quickly from the tov (good) land which <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is giving you.
> 'And you shall lay up these words of Mine in your heart and in your being, and shall bind them as a sign on your hand, and they shall be as frontlets between your eyes.
> 'And you shall teach them to your children, speaking of them when you sit in your house, and when you walk by the way, and when you lie down, and when you rise up.
> And shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates,
> So that your chai (life) and the chai (life) of your children are increased on the soil of which <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> swore to your fathers to give them, as the yomim (days) of the shamayim (heavens) on the erets (earth).

**Bemidbar (Num 15:37-41)**

> And <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> spoke to Mosheh (Moses), saying,
> "Speak to the children of Yisra'Yah, and you shall say to them to make tzitzit (fringes) on the corners of their garments throughout their generations, and to put a blue cord in the tzitzit of the corners.
> "And it shall be to you for a tzitzit, and you shall see it, and shall remember all the commands of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> and shall do them, and not search after your own heart and your own eyes after which you went whoring,
> So that you remember, and shall do all My commands, and be set-apart to your Abba.
> "I am <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba, who brought you out of the land of Mitsrayim (Egypt), to be your Abba. I am <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> your Abba."

**Leader:** Barukh shem k'vod malkhuto l'olam va-ed.

**Congregation:** Praised be His name, whose esteemed reign is forever and ever. Amein.

## Tehillim 118 to 122

**Tehillim 118**

1 O give thanks to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, for He is good! For His loving-commitment is everlasting.
2 Let Yisra'Yah now say, "His loving-commitment is everlasting."
3 Let the house of Aharon now say, "His loving-commitment is everlasting."
4 Let those who fear <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> now say, "His loving-commitment is everlasting."
5 I called on Yah in distress; Yah answered me in a broad place.
6 <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is on my side; I do not fear what man does to me!
7 <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is for me among those helping me; therefore I look on those hating me.
8 It is better to take refuge in <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> than to trust in man.
9 It is better to take refuge in <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> than to trust in princes.
10 All the gentiles surrounded me, in the Name of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> I shall cut them off.
11 They surrounded me, yes, they surrounded me; in the Name of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> shall I cut them off.
12 They surrounded me like bees; they were extinguished like burning thorns; in the Name of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> I shall cut them off.
13 Pushing, they pushed me to fall, but <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> helped me.
14 Yah is my strength and song, and He has become my deliverance.
15 The voice of rejoicing and deliverance is in the tents of the righteous; the right hand of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is doing mightily.
16 The right hand of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is exalted, the right hand of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> does mightily.
17 Let me not die, but live, and declare the works of Yah.
18 Yah has punished me severely, but did not give me over to death.
19 Open to me the gates of righteousness; I enter through them, I thank Yah.
20 This is the gate of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, the righteous enter through it.
21 I thank You, for You have answered me, and have become my deliverance.
22 The stone which the builders rejected has become the chief corner-stone.
23 This was from <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, it is marvellous in our eyes.
24 This is the day <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> has made, let us rejoice and be glad in it.
25 I pray, O <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, please save us now; I pray, O <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, please send prosperity.
26 Blessed is He who is coming in the Name of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>! We shall bless you from the House of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>.
27 <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> is Mighty, and He gave us light; bind the festival offering with cords to the horns of the slaughter-place.
28 You are my Mighty One, and I praise You; You are my Abba, I exalt You.
29 Give thanks to <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>, for He is good! For His loving-commitment is everlasting.

**Tehillim 122**

1 I was glad when they said to me, "Let us go into the House of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>."
2 Our feet have been standing within your gates, O Yahushalayim!
3 Yahushalayim is built as a city that is bound together,
4 Where the tribes have come up, the tribes of Yah, a witness to Yisra'Yah, to give thanks to the Name of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span>.
5 For there the thrones of right-ruling were set, the thrones of the house of Dawid.
6 Pray for the peace of Yahushalayim, let those who love You be at rest.
7 Peace be within your walls, rest in your citadels.
8 For the sake of my brothers and companions, I say, "Shalom (peace) be within you."
9 For the sake of the House of <span dir="auto">יהוה</span> our Abba I seek your tov (good).
