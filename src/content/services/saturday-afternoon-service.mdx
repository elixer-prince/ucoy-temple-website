The Shabbat afternoon abodah is the reading and study of the Torah kept later in
the day, with the blessings that surround the reading of the Haftarah.

## Reading and Study of the Torah

The Torah is read aloud from the weekly portion, and the whole passage may be
read responsively by the congregation.

## Torah Berakah (Before)

**Leader:** Bar’chu et Yahweh ham’vorach baruch Yahweh ham’vorach l’olam va’ed baruch atah yahweh shadday melech ha’olam asher bachar banu mikol ha’amim v’natan lanu et torato baruch atah Yahweh notein hatorah.

**Congregation:** Praise the One to whom our praise is due! Praised be the One to whom our praise is due, now and forever! We praise You, Yahweh All-Mighty, Sovereign of the universe: You have called us to Your service by giving us the Torah. We praise You, Yahweh, Giver of the Torah.

## Torah Berakah (After)

**Leader:** Baruch atah Yahweh Abba melech ha’olam asher natan lanu torat emet v’chayei olam nata b’tocheinu baruch atah Yahweh notein hatorah.

**Congregation:** We praise You, Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the Universe: You have given us a Torah of truth, implanting within us eternal life. We praise You, Yahweh, Giver of the Torah.

## Haftarah Berakah (Before)

**Leader:** Baruch atah Yahweh Abba melech ha’olam asher bachar binvi’im tovim v’ratza v’divreihem hane’emarim be’emet baruch atah yahweh habocheir batorah uv’mosheh avdo uv’yisrayah amo, uvin’vi’ei ha’emet vatzadek.

**Congregation:** We praise You, Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the universe: You have called faithful prophets to speak words of truth. We praise You, Yahweh, for the revelation of Torah, for Mosheh Your servant and Yisra’Yah Your people, and for the prophets of truth and righteousness.

## Haftarah Berakah (After)

**Leader:** Baruch atah Yahweh Abba melech ha’olam tzur kol ha’olamim tzadik b’chol hadorot, ha’eil hane’eman ha’omeir v’oseh ham’dabeir um’kayeim, shekol d’varav emet vatzadek al hatorah, v’al ha’avodah, v’al han’vi’im v’al yom ha’shabbat hazeh shenatata lanu Yahweh shadday lik’dusha v’lin’nucha, l’chavod ul’thif’aret al hakol, Yahweh Shadday aachnu modem lach um’var’chim otach yit’barach shim’cha b’fi kol chai tamid l’olam va’ed. Baruch atah yahweh, m’kadeis ha’shabbat.

**Congregation:** We praise You, Yahweh our Abba, Sovereign of the universe, the Rock of all creation, the Righteous One of all generations, the faithful Yahweh whose word is deed, whose every command is just and true. For the Torah, for the privilege of worship, for the prophets, and for this Shabbat yom \{day\} that You, our Yahweh All-Mighty, have given us to be set apart and rest, for abundance and honour, we thank and praise You. May Your name be praised forever by every living being. We praise You, Yahweh All-Mighty, for the Shabbat and its sacred-ness.

## The Shabbat Afternoon Abodah

The Sabbath afternoon service ends where the evening begins. The Sabbath is
closed after dark with Havdalah, and its order is on the
[closing of Shabbat](/services/closing-of-shabbat) page.

The full order, with every prayer written out, is in the
