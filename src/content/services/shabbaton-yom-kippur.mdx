## Teshuvah

The Hebrew term teshuvah is used to refer to "repentance". This implies that transgression and sin are the natural and inevitable consequence of man's straying from Yahweh and His Torah (law), and that it is man's destiny and duty to be with Yahweh.

HAVDALAH RITUAL (the end of Shabbat)

**Congregation:** Yahweh is my teshuvah \{deliverance\}, I trust and have no fear; Yahweh is my strength and my song, and has become my Yesha.

**Leader:** Yahweh will give strength to his people, Yahweh will barak his children with Shalom \{Peace\}. Shalom to him who is a far off, and to him who is near, says Yahweh.

**Congregation:** I will exalt the Torah; I will call upon the name of Yahweh.

**Leader:** Ba-rukh a- tah Yahweh Ab-ba me-lekh ha-o-lam, bo-rei p’ri ha-ga-fen.

**Congregation:** praise are You, Yahweh our Father, Sovereign of the universe, who created the fruit of the vine.

**Leader:** Ba-rukh a-tah Yahweh Ab-ba me-lekh ha-o-lam, bo-rei mi-nei ve sa-mim.

**Congregation:** praise are You, Yahweh our Father, Sovereign of the universe, who created various spices.

**Leader:** Ba-rukh a-tah Yahweh Ab-ba me-lekh ha-o-lam, bo-rei mo-rei ha-eish.

**Congregation:** praise are You, Yahweh our Father, Sovereign of the universe, who created the illumination of the fire.

**Leader:** Ba-rukh a-tah Yahweh Ab-ba me-lekh ha-o-lam palah ba-yin qadosh chol bay-in or cha-shek ba-yin yom-shabat yom-vav a-mal ezer “us” anach-nu palah ba-yin e-meth a-mad he-bel shav agab che-sed to-qeph na-cha-lah tub sha-bu-a amets emun sha-bu-a arukah shalu sha-bu-a bera-kah shalom.

**Congregation:** praise are You, Yahweh our Father, Sovereign of the universe, who separated between the sacred and the profane, between light and darkness, between the seventh yom and the six yomim of labor. Help us too, we pray to distinguish between that which is real and enduring, and that which is fleeting and vain. May we value affection and helpfulness more than power and possessions, that the simchah of a tov (good) week, a week of courage and commitment, a week of health and prosperity, a week of berakah and Shalom \{peace\}. Hallelu’Yah!

**Congregation:** Yahweh barak \{bless\} you and keep you; Yahweh make His face shine upon you and be tov \{good\} to you; Yahweh lifts up His countenance upon you and give you Shalom \{Peace\}.
