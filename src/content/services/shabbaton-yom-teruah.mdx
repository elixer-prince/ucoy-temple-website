Yom Teruah is the first of the appointed times of the seventh month, a High
Sabbath kept with the sounding of the shofar and a holy convocation. This page
carries the order of that day.

## The sounding of the shofar

The day is named for the blast of the shofar. We read the passages that command
it, then the shofar is sounded in its long and broken calls for all to hear.

### The readings of the day

The readings set the day apart: the birth of Yitschaq, the binding on the
mountain, and the promise that the Eternal remembers His covenant.

## The holy convocation

We gather again for prayer and a shared meal. No work is done, as the day is a
Sabbath of rest, and the day closes with Havdalah when the first stars appear.

Dates for Yom Teruah are given on the temple calendar, because the day follows
the Hebrew month of Tishrei rather than a fixed date.
