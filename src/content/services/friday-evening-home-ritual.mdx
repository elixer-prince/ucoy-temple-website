The Sabbath is welcomed in the home on Friday evening. This ritual is kept around
the family table rather than in the sanctuary, so anyone can keep it: there are no
words to learn by heart and nothing to prepare beyond a cup, a cover for the
candles, and the evening meal.

## Lighting the Shabbat candles

The Sabbath is welcomed as the sun sets. Two candles are lit, and the blessing is
said over them. Where two candles are not kept, one is lit, and the words are the
same.

<div lang="he" dir="rtl">

אשר קידשנו במצותיו וצונו להבדיל בין הקדש ובין החול

</div>

The English of the same words:

> As He commanded us and instructed us to distinguish between the sacred and the
> ordinary.

We sing the song of the Sabbath after the candles are lit. The blessing ends with
the words <span dir="auto">שבת קודש</span>, and from that point the evening is
Shabbat.

## Shabbat Rest

**Shabbat Berakah**

Vay'hi erev vay'hi voker yom hashish vay'khulu hashamayim v'ha'aretz v'khol tz'va'am
vay'khal Yahweh bayom hash'vi'i m'la'kh'to asher asah vayish'bot bayom hash'vi'i mikol m'la'kh'to asher asah Vay'varekh Yahweh et yom hash'vi'i vay'kadeish oto ki vo shavat mikol m'la'kh'to asher bara Yahweh la'asot.

And there was evening and there was morning, a sixth yom (day). The heavens and the earth were finished, the whole host of them. And on the seventh yom (day) Yahweh completed his work that he had done, and he rested on the seventh yom from all his work that he had done. And Yahweh baruk (blessed) the seventh yom, and set it apart because in it, he had rested from all his work that Yahweh had created.

Barukh atah Yahweh, Ab-ba, melekh ha-olam asher kid'shanu b'mitz'votav v'ratzah vanu
v'shabat kad'sho b'ahavah uv'ratzon hin'chilanu zikaron l'ma'aseih v'rei'shit ki hu yom t'chilah l'mik'ra'ei kodesh zeikher litzi'at Mitz'rayim ki vanu vachar'ta v'otanu kidash'ta mikol ha'amim
v'shabat kad'sh'kha b'ahavah uv'ratzon hin'chal'tanu. Barukh atah Yahweh m'kadeish hashabat.

Praised are You Yahweh, our Ab-ba, Sovereign of the Universe who set apart us with his commandments, and has been pleased with us. You have lovingly and willingly given us Your set apart Shabbat as an inheritance, in memory of creation because it is the first yom of our set apart assemblies, in memory of the departure from Mitzrayim because You have chosen us and made us set apart from all peoples and have willingly and lovingly given us Your set apart Shabbat for an inheritance. Praised are You Yahweh, who set apart Shabbat.

## The Berakah (Blessing)

### Berakah (Blessing) for a Son

Ye’simcha Yahweh ke’Ephraim ve’chi’menashe

May Yahweh make you like Ephraim and Menashe.

### Berakah (Blessing) for a Daughter

Ye’simech Yahweh ke’Sarah, Rivka, Rach’Yah, ve’Leah

May Yahweh make you like Sarah, Rivka, Rachel and Leah

### Berakah (Blessing) for a Husband

Mi ha-ish, he-hafetz hayim, ohev yamim lir’ot tov? N’tzor l’shon’kha mei’ra, u’sfotecha mi-daber mirma. Sur mei’ra va’aseh tov, bakesh shalom v’rod’feih

Who is the man who is eager for life, who hopes for long yomim (days) of tov (good) fortune? He guards his tongue from speaking deceit, he turns from evil and does tov (good), he desires shalom (peace) and pursues it. Tehillim (Psalm 34)

### Berakah (Blessing) for a Wife

Eishet Chayil mi yimtza, v’rakhok mi’pninim michrah. Batakh bah lev ba’alah, v’shalal lo yekhsar. Piha pat’cha v’chochmah, v’torat chesed al l’shonah. Kamu vaneha v’ya’ashruhah, ba’alah vay’hal’lah. Rabot banot asu chayil, v’at alit al kulanah.

A woman of valour, who can find? Her worth is far beyond rubies. Her husband trusts in her and lacks for nothing. Her lips are full of wisdom, her mouth with loving-kindness. Her children rise and bless her, her husband sings her praises: “Many women have done well, but you surpass them all!” (Proverbs 31)

## Kiddush

### Berakah (Blessing Wine)

Kiddush is recited while holding a cup of wine or grape juice, no less than 3.3 ounces. The wine or grape juice is held in the right hand while standing when reciting the Barakah (blessing). Drink the wine after the Barakah is complete.

Barukh atah Yahweh, Ab-ba, melekh ha-olam
borei p'ri hagafen

Praised are you, Yahweh, our Father, Sovereign of the universe
Who creates the fruit of the vine.

### Ha-Motzi: Berakah (Blessing Bread)

Before eating, the head of the household removes the cover from the two challah loaves, lifting them while reciting the following blessing.

Barukh atah Yahweh, Ab-ba, melekh ha-olam hamotzi lechem min ha'aretz.

Praised are You, Yahweh, our Father, Sovereign of the Universe
who brings forth bread from the earth.

The challah is then ripped into pieces or sliced and passed around the table, so that each person may have a piece. The family meal may then begin.

## Grace After Meal

Praised is He, and praised be His Name.

Praised are You, O Yahweh our Father, sovereign of the universe, who feeds the whole world with His goodness, with grace, with loving kindness and with mercy.

He gives food to all flesh for His kindness endures forever. And in His great goodness we have always never lacked, and may we never lack, food for ever and ever, and does good to all, and prepares food for all His creatures which He created.

Praise are You, O Yahweh who provides food for all. Yahweh will give strength to His people, Yahweh will barak (bless) His people with shalom (peace)

## Reading and study of the Torah

The portion for the week is read aloud, with the blessings before and after. The old
page pointed readers at
[chabad.org for the Torah portion of the week](https://www.chabad.org/parshah/default_cdo/jewish/Torah-Portion.htm),
and that link is kept here.

### Torah Berakah (Before)

**Leader:** BAR’CHU ET YAHWEH HAM’VORACH BARUCH YAHWEH HAM’VORACH L’OLAM VA’ED BARUCH ATAH YAHWEH SHADDAY MELECH HA’OLAM ASHER BACHAR BANU MIKOL HA’AMIM V’NATAN LANU ET TORATO BARUCH ATAH YAHWEH NOTEIN HA-TORAH

**Congregation:** Praise the One to whom our praise is due! Praised be the One to whom our praise is due, now and forever! We praise You, Yahweh All-Mighty, Sovereign of the universe: You have called us to Your service by giving us the Torah. We praise You, Yahweh, Giver of the Torah.

### Torah Berakah (After)

**Leader:** BARUCH ATAH YAHWEH ABBA MELECH HA’OLAM ASHER NATAN LANU TORAT EMET V’CHAYEI OLAM NATA B’TOCHEINU BARUCH ATAH YAHWEH NOTEIN HATORAH

**Congregation:** We praise You, Yahweh our Father, Sovereign of the Universe: You have given us a Torah of truth, implanting within us eternal life. We praise You, Yahweh, Giver of the Torah.

## Yahweh’s Berakah

**Leader:** Yahweh spoke to Mosheh saying speak to Aaron saying speak to the children of Yisra’Yah and this is what you shall say to them

Yishvarech’cha Yahweh va’yish’ma’recha.Ya-eir Yahweh panav eilecha vichunecha.Yissa Yahweh panav eilecha v’yaseim l’cha shalom.

> May Yahweh bless you and keep you. May Yahweh make His face shine upon you and be tov (good) to you. May Yahweh lift up His countenance upon you and give you Shalom. (Peace)

## Keeping it in a household

The ritual is kept the same way in every home, so a guest who arrives at any
house during the week joins in without being asked. The congregation keeps the
Shabbat together at the building on the occasions when the calendar says so, and
those times are given on the temple calendar and in the announcements on the
home page.

The full order, with every prayer written out, is in the
