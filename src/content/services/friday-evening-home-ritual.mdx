The Sabbath is welcomed in the home on Friday evening. This ritual is kept around
the family table rather than in the sanctuary, so anyone can keep it: there are no
words to learn by heart and nothing to prepare beyond a cup, a cover for the
candles, and the evening meal. The order below is the order it is kept in, so you
can follow along from a phone.

## Lighting the Shabbat candles

The ritual begins as the sun sets. Two candles are lit, and the blessing is said
over them. Where two candles are not kept, one is lit, and the words are the
same.

<div lang="he" dir="rtl">

אשר קידשנו במצותיו וצונו להבדיל בין הקדש ובין החול

</div>

The English of the same words:

> As He commanded us and instructed us to distinguish between the sacred and the
> ordinary.

We sing the song of the Sabbath after the candles are lit. The blessing ends with
the words <span dir="auto">שבת קודש</span>, and from that point the evening is
Shabbat.

## The blessing over the cup

After the candles, the blessing is said over the first cup of the evening. Bread
is broken and shared, and the blessing is said over that too. Where wine is not
kept, the blessing over the grape juice is said in the same words, and the
ritual continues unchanged.

## Reading and teaching

Once the meal has begun, the family reads from the Torah portion of the week. The
reading is short, read aloud with the English alongside, and followed by a few
words of teaching from the Testimony. A child of the household reads aloud if
they wish to.

## The Sabbath meal

The rest of the evening is the meal itself and the company kept at the table. No
work is done from here until after dark on Saturday night, when the Sabbath is
closed with Havdalah.

## Keeping it in a household

The ritual is kept the same way in every home, so a guest who arrives at any
house during the week joins in without being asked. The congregation keeps the
Shabbat together at the building on the occasions when the calendar says so, and
those times are given on the temple calendar and in the announcements on the
home page.
