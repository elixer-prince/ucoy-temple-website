# Shabbat Morning Service — Regular Schedule Resumes

Beginning this week, the Saturday morning Shacharit service returns to
its regular **9:30 AM** start time.

## What to expect

- Opening prayers in Hebrew and English
- Reading from this week's Torah portion
- A short teaching from the Testimony
- Kiddush fellowship after the service

## Location reminder

We meet at our regular building:
**United Congregation of Yisra'Yah**
123 Fellowship Lane

All visitors are welcome. Whether you have attended before or this is
your first visit, we look forward to worshiping with you.

## Upcoming readings

This week's parashah is **Va'etchanan** (Deuteronomy 3:23–7:11), which
continues the final discourses of Mosheh.
